01 / About me

Visuals with a little nerve and a clear reason to exist.

G.

I’m Gargi Tripathi, a visual designer based in Ahmedabad and working remotely wherever a good idea needs a better outfit.

I’m drawn to work that has contrast: a dark, editorial edge with enough restraint to keep the message in charge. I like making presentations feel considered, social posts feel less disposable, and small visual details do a surprising amount of heavy lifting.

02 / Work ethic

Beautiful is a given. Useful is non-negotiable.

A / 01

Make it make sense first.

I start with hierarchy, not decoration. If the story is fuzzy, no amount of polish is going to rescue it.

B / 02

Details are the difference.

The line break, the icon weight, the extra breath around a headline — small decisions are where the whole thing starts to feel right.

C / 03

Keep it moving.

I’m thoughtful without being precious. Clear feedback, neat files, honest timelines and work that holds up when someone else opens it.
